Right Whale Sponsorship Updates

For more than 45 years, the New England Aquarium’s Right Whale Research Program has been at the forefront of right whale research since a remnant population was discovered in 1980. Our program is the longest-running and most comprehensive North Atlantic right whale research and conservation initiative in the world. In addition to maintaining the North Atlantic Right Whale Catalog, we are investigating solutions to reduce the threats of boat strikes and fishing gear entanglements.


Thank you for your sponsorship of a North Atlantic right whale! Your support helps our scientists monitor their population status, habitat use, calving rates, and supports our work to inform and implement effective management initiatives to save this critically endangered species.
